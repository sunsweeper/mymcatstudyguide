RAISE YOUR HAND

When a concept won’t click, ask how someone else remembers it.

Raise Your Hand is designed to connect questions and memory strategies to the exact flashcard you are studying—without sending you into a general discussion feed.

The idea

Help where you need it.

01 / ASK

Ask a card-specific question

When a card isn’t sticking, open Raise Your Hand to ask about that concept without attaching your name to the question.

02 / DISCOVER

Explore previous explanations

Look through other students’ approaches to the same concept, including mnemonics, explanations, and memory tricks.

03 / SHARE

Offer what worked for you

Share an explanation that helped you remember a difficult concept. Useful contributions may help someone else work through the same challenge.
