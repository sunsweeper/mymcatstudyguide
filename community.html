How it works

Help where you need it.

01 / ASK

Raise your hand on a card

Tap ✋ Raise Your Hand while you study. The card and the date are filled in for you. Add your own question, or send ours: “I’m having a hard time with this one. Anyone want to share how they remember this?”

02 / NOTIFY

It goes to students who know it

Your question goes to the bell inbox of every student who has gotten that card right twice in a row. Your question shows as Pending until one of them answers.

03 / LEARN

See how they remember it

You get a notification when someone answers. Every answered question on a card stays there for the next student, and you can mark the answer that helped.

If no one answers

Rosalind steps in.

If no other student who meets the requirement has answered your question within a few days, Rosalind, our AI study companion, will step in. We really think an answer from your peers helps the most, so we give them the chance first.

Anonymous, and looked after.

Questions and answers never show your name. Anything can be reported with one click, and the TabiPrep team reviews every report. Raise Your Hand is for TabiPrep cards; cards you import stay private to you.
