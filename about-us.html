About TabiPrep

Every meaningful journey has people behind it.

Tabi (旅) is the Japanese word for journey. I picked it because that’s what studying for something hard really is: a long road, taken one step at a time.

TabiPrep started at my kitchen table, with a simple question: why do flashcards make you feel like you’re learning when so much of it doesn’t stick?

The answer, it turns out, is well studied. So I built TabiPrep around what the research says actually works:

You write every answer from memory. Pulling an answer out of your own head is called retrieval practice, and it’s one of the most reliable findings in learning science: it builds memory far better than rereading or recognizing an answer on a flipped card. That’s why TabiPrep asks you to write before you flip.

AI grades what you meant, not the words you used. Rating yourself is easy to fudge, especially when you’re tired. Your answer is first checked against the card’s key concepts, and when that isn’t a clear match, an AI model reads it for meaning, tells you what you got right and what was missing, and lets you dispute any grade. An honest grade means an honest schedule.

Each card comes back right before you’d forget it. TabiPrep schedules reviews with FSRS, a modern spaced-repetition model that estimates how strong each memory is and when it’s likely to fade. Cards you know well step back, cards you’re about to lose come forward, and the whole plan bends to fit your test date.

Misses get extra work, right away. When you struggle with a card, you write the answer in your own words before moving on. Putting it in your own words is called the generation effect, and it makes the correction stick. That card then comes back sooner until it does.

Progress you can trust. Your weakness dashboard looks at how well your answers hold up a day or more later, not how many cards you’ve clicked through, so it shows where your next hour should actually go.

But no one walks a road like this alone.

Behind every student grinding through late nights, there are people who make it possible. Parents who believed in you early. Friends who quizzed you, fed you, or just sat with you while you studied. Partners who carried a little more so you could carry this. Mentors who saw something in you before you saw it yourself.

That’s love, and it’s part of every hard journey worth taking. TabiPrep was built with that in mind: to make the most of the time you have, so you can give a little more of it back to the people who help you get there.

If you’re here, you’re probably on a long road too. Maybe you’re doing it for the science, for the prestige, for the people you’ll serve someday, or for someone who believed in you before you did.

Whatever brought you here, I hope this makes the road a little easier.

It’s your journey. Let’s make every step count.

— Aaron, founder
