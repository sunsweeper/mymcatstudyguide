About TabiPrep

It started with one person.

Tabi (旅) is the Japanese word for journey. I picked it because that’s what this was, and still is.

TabiPrep didn’t start as a company. It started at my kitchen table, building flashcards for someone I loved who was studying for the MCAT. Someone who changed my life. She was working toward medicine with a kind of determination I’d never been around, and watching her grind through late nights made me want to help in the only way I knew how: by building something.

So I built it. Not a deck of flashcards, but a better way to study them. One where you write the answer out instead of guessing. One that grades what you actually understood, not what you recognized. One that brings each card back right before you’d forget it, so none of those late-night hours go to waste.

She’s on her own journey now, and so am I. That’s how journeys go sometimes. The road splits, and you learn that someone can change your life without staying in it. I’m grateful for her, and I genuinely wish her the best on the road she’s chosen.

What she inspired didn’t leave with her. It became this.

If you’re here, you’re probably on a long road too. Maybe you’re doing it for the science, for the prestige, for the people you’ll care for someday, or for someone who believed in you before you did.

Whatever brought you here, I hope this makes the road a little easier.

It’s your journey. Let’s make every step count.

— Aaron, founder
