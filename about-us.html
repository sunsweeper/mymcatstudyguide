Why we built it

TabiPrep started as a study tool for one person. It was built on a simple idea: recognizing an answer when you flip a card is not the same as knowing it. More cards aren’t the answer. Getting more out of each card is.

How TabiPrep works

  • Write it before you see it. Active Recall asks you to put the answer in your own words before you flip the card, because pulling an answer out of memory is what makes it stick.
  • Get an honest grade. Your answer is checked against the card’s key concepts, and Rosalind, our AI study companion, grades anything that needs a closer look and tells you exactly what was missing.
  • See each card at the right time. A spaced-repetition schedule brings cards back just before you’re likely to forget them, so your hours go to what you need most.
  • Know where you stand. Your dashboard tracks your study time, what you knew, and where your weak spots are across the MCAT content areas.

What we believe

More learning per card, not more cards. Students preparing for medicine give up a great deal to get there. Their study time should be spent on understanding, not on busywork.
