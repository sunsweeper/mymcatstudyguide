## 3. Retrieval variety

- **Question types:** 686 of 1000 cards are straight recall, 274 test conceptual understanding, and only 40 ask the student to apply knowledge to a scenario or data. The MCAT is mostly passage- and scenario-based (AAMC: Skills 2–4 = 65% of each science section), so application cards are the biggest retrieval gap.
- **Reverse recall** exists on 257 cards (26%).
- **Graphical and experimental cards:** none exist. These topics already have recall cards that describe a graph in words, so a graphical card would add a genuinely different skill:

  - **1A:** Michaelis–Menten and Lineweaver–Burk plots with each inhibitor type (1A-017–1A-021, 1A-037)
  - **1A/3B:** Hemoglobin–oxygen dissociation curve and its shifts (3B-020, 3B-035)
  - **5A:** Titration curves: weak acid/strong base, polyprotic acids, buffer region (5A-011, 5A-014)
  - **5E:** Reaction energy diagrams with and without a catalyst; phase diagrams (5E-002, 5E-003, 5E-011)
  - **4B/5E:** PV diagrams and gas-law graphs (5E-025, 4B-015)
  - **3A:** Action potential voltage trace with the channel events (3A-013, 3A-016)
  - **3B:** ECG trace labeling (3B-031–3B-033); sarcomere band diagram (3B-001)
  - **3B:** Nephron diagram with transport by segment (3B-019, 3B-044)
  - **4D:** Ray diagrams for mirrors and lenses (4D-009, 4D-029)
  - **4D:** NMR and IR spectra interpretation (4D-001, 4D-002, 4D-014)
  - **9B:** Population pyramids and demographic transition stages (9B-007, 9B-016, 9B-019)
  - **6A/6B:** Signal detection matrix; serial position curve (6A-015, 6B-009)
  - **S4:** Error bars, confidence intervals and scatterplots (S4-008, S4-018)
  - **1D:** Pathway maps: glycolysis/gluconeogenesis bypass steps, ETC complexes (1D-025, 1D-034)

- **Experimental-data cards:** short "here is a result, what does it show" cards for S3/S4 and for biochemistry methods (5C blots and chromatography, 1B PCR and sequencing).
