MCAT prep · How it works

How TabiPrep works for the MCAT.

Write each answer from memory, get it graded by Rosalind, and see every card again at the right time. Here’s what happens behind each card.

A smarter way to prepare

The science behind every study session.

Five capabilities working together to make your preparation more focused, more personal, and easier to manage.

01 / RETRIEVE
↗

Active Recall

Remembering an answer strengthens learning more than simply rereading it. Our flashcards ask you to retrieve the information first, then check your understanding.

The benefit: practice remembering, not just recognizing.
02 / REMEMBER
◷

Intelligently Timed Reviews

Instead of building your own review calendar, the system schedules concepts to return at intervals tailored to your performance and upcoming test date.

The benefit: spend more time learning and less time planning.
03 / ADAPT
✧

Adaptive Review

Material you struggle with receives extra attention, while concepts you consistently remember are revisited less often—without disappearing from your study plan.

The benefit: focus on the gaps that need you most.
04 / UNDERSTAND
▥

Progress You Can See

Go beyond counting completed cards. See which concepts you've encountered, what you've retained across reviews, and where the evidence is still developing.

The benefit: know what to work on next—and why.
05 / STAY FOCUSED
◎

Aligned With the AAMC Content Outline

Study material is organized around the AAMC's published MCAT content outline, helping you connect individual cards to the broader topics you're preparing to master.

The benefit: keep your study time connected to the exam.
YOUR STUDY PARTNER
♥︎

Intelligent Flashcards

Active recall, intelligently timed reviews, and adaptive learning work together behind the scenes. You focus on the next card; the system helps guide what comes after it.

The benefit: a study experience that gets more useful as you use it.

Progress that means something

Know your weaknesses.
Know what to do about them.

Your actual dashboard doesn't pretend that a few correct answers equal mastery. It looks at your ratings, how knowledge holds up over time, and how much evidence you have in each subject.

The more you study, the clearer the picture becomes—and the easier it is to decide where your next hour should go.

The dashboard shown is based on your real interface design, with fictional, more-developed activity data for demonstration only.
Study without losing your place

Switch devices, keep your place

Your progress is saved to your account. Sign in on another device and TabiPrep asks whether to end the session on the first one and continue here. Say yes and you pick up with everything you’ve saved; the other device signs out. One device studies at a time, so your progress never overwrites itself.
