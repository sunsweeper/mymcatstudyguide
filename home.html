OUR TECHNOLOGY

Learning science, built into every card.

Each idea below comes from research on how memory works. TabiPrep puts all of them into one study session, so you don't have to manage any of it yourself.

01 / RECALL

Write it from memory

Pulling an answer out of memory strengthens it far more than rereading it. Every card asks you to write your answer before you flip it.

02 / GRADE

Graded by AI

Our team of AI study companions reads what you wrote and grades the meaning, not the exact wording. You see what was missing, and you can dispute any grade.

03 / SPACE

Each card on its own clock

A modern spaced-repetition model schedules every card's next review for when you're about to forget it, and fits the plan to your test date.

04 / ADAPT

Misses get extra work

Struggled with a card? You write the answer in your own words right away, and the card comes back sooner, until it sticks.

05 / SEE

Honest progress

A weakness dashboard shows which topics need your time, based on how well your memory is holding up, not on how many cards you've clicked through.

06 / YOURS

Bring your own cards

Import your Anki or CSV decks and study them with the same system. Your decks stay private to you.
