A clear view of your data ✳
Draft for review — not a published legal policy. This page describes intended categories and practices for a student study platform. The operator must verify actual data collection, service providers, retention periods, security, and legal requirements before publication. No statement here should be treated as confirmation of live implementation.

Draft date: October 26, 2026 · Website: TabiPrep.com · Operator: JSA Knowledge & Cattle LLC

1. Scope

This draft addresses the TabiPrep website, student accounts, study sessions, and related support or community features. It does not cover third-party sites reached through external links.

2. Information the service may process

  • Account information: email address, login credentials or authentication identifiers, and account preferences.
  • Study activity: cards viewed, written answers, self-ratings, bookmarks, notes, review schedules, progress indicators, and study-session history.
  • Community contributions: questions, responses, and reports submitted through Raise Your Hand, if the feature launches.
  • Support requests: contact details and the content of messages sent through a connected contact channel.
  • Technical information: device/browser details, service logs, and cookies or similar technologies as required to operate, secure, or improve the service.

Exactly which categories are collected, which are optional, and which third parties receive them must be confirmed from the live implementation.

3. Why information may be used

Potential purposes include authenticating accounts, delivering study sessions, scheduling reviews, maintaining progress, answering support requests, preventing abuse, improving reliability, and administering community content if enabled.

4. Service providers and sharing

Hosting, authentication, database, analytics, email, and payment vendors may process information on the operator’s behalf. The final policy must identify applicable provider categories, relevant disclosures, and any separate uses. This draft does not claim that student information is never shared or sold; those practices require verification.

5. Retention and deletion

Retention periods and deletion workflows must be defined and documented before launch. The final policy should explain how long account, study, support, and community information is kept, including backup and legal-retention exceptions.

6. Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies or local storage may support sign-in and functionality. Any optional analytics, marketing trackers, or consent choices must be disclosed accurately based on the live website.

7. Security

The operator should use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. The final policy should reflect the actual security and incident-response practices in place.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, students may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about personal data. The final policy must describe the available request channel, verification process, and any applicable state-specific disclosures, including California requirements where relevant.

9. Community privacy

If Raise Your Hand becomes available, the final policy should explain what “anonymous” means, what other users and moderators can see, and whether content can remain visible after account deletion. Do not assume anonymous display means the service itself has no record of the account associated with a post.

10. Children and age eligibility

The intended minimum age, any parental-consent requirements, and the handling of underage users’ information must be established and disclosed before launch.

11. Changes to this policy

The final policy should state how material changes are announced and provide an effective date.

12. Contact the operator

JSA Knowledge & Cattle LLC operates TabiPrep. The official privacy-contact email and any required postal address must be added before publication. In the meantime, see the Contact UsAbout Rosalind preview page.
